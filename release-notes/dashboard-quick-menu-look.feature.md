- **One quick menu and one battery picker for every dashboard theme, in Rotorflight's colors, with a rotary style (`widgets/dashboard/fullscreen_menu.lua`, `menu_look.lua`, `menu_roll.lua`, `menu_roll_sources.lua`, `battery_pick_menu.lua`, `battery_profile_menu.lua`, `views.lua`, `themes/{urban,grid,deck}`, `lib/battery_pick.lua`, `app/pages/settings/general/page.lua`, `app/pages/settings/dashboard/quick_menu/page.lua`, `docs/dashboard/quick-menu.md`)**:
  - The quick menu, the battery picker, the battery profile page and the erase question draw in a light or a dark scheme instead of the radio's theme colors, the same on every theme: *Settings* > *General* > *Appearance*, light as it comes. Urban, Grid and Deck no longer draw these pages themselves.
  - ERASE BLACKBOX shows how full the blackbox is on every theme, not only on Urban, Grid and Deck.
  - *Settings* > *General* > *Appearance* offers a *Menu style* for the quick menu and the battery picker: *Card*, the buttons as before, or *Rotary*, rolling lists for the wheel or a finger: a swipe rolls, a tap on the middle row acts.
  - The battery picker shows cycles as well, in two columns from two packs up, and scrolls where more packs exist than fit, so every pack can be reached.
  - With *Set the pack's battery profile* on, a pack with no clear profile on this board is listed but cannot be picked, and a pick is recorded only once the flight controller has answered the profile write; a write that fails says *NOT SAVED* and leaves the pack before it in force.

- **The flight controller's answer to an in-flight tuning step survives a decode backlog (`tasks/events/telemetry_bg/drain.lua`)**: after a long widget pass the dashboard's drain decodes only the newest frames of the backlog, and the answer to a touch step fell among the dropped ones, so the new value and its announcement came only with the flight controller's repeat about three seconds later. For three seconds after a step the dropped frames are searched for that answer and it is published ahead of the kept frames; outside that window, and with the background decoder (`rfsbg`), nothing changes.

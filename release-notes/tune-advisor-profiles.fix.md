- **Tune Advisor keeps and combines flights per PID and rate profile (`lib/tune_history.lua`, `tasks/events/tune_capture.lua`, `flight_tuning/tune_advisor`)**: the page shows the flights of the profiles active on the flight controller, combining up to the last 5 flown on them; flights on other profiles, or on profiles not known, are passed over instead of being combined or ending the count, and stay saved for their own profiles. The history keeps the last 5 flights per profile, at most 20 in all and under the read limit. Without the PID# and RTE# telemetry sensors the radio asks the flight controller for its profiles before a flight is saved and when the page opens or reloads, only while disarmed. The page says "No flight saved on PID 2, rates 1 yet" when the active profiles have none, the first reason notes that flights on other profiles are kept, and the help's `Profiles` line says that only the active profiles' flights count (#1166).

- **In-flight tuning refuses a board whose slots use the tuning channels only in part, and reads a continuous always-on slot as the flight controller does (`widgets/dashboard/inflight/prime.lua`, `widgets/dashboard/inflight/drive.lua`, `widgets/dashboard/inflight/screen.lua`, `docs/dashboard/inflight-tuning.md`)**:
  - An adjustment slot that touches the bank and value channels in any arrangement other than bank and value, such as a slot on the value channel enabled by another switch or a slot enabled by the bank channel that reads another channel, refuses the whole set: no parameter is offered, and the ground screen names the slot (**Slot 40 blocks tuning**) and says to change it in the Configurator. Before, such a slot was ignored and the flight controller stepped it beside the parameter on the screen (#811).
  - A continuous slot that is always on and reads the value channel now refuses a step value that lies within the flight controller's margin around its first range, and its second range, which the flight controller never reads, no longer refuses anything.

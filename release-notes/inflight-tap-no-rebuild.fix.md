- **In-flight tuning: a tap on + or - no longer rebuilds the value surface (`widgets/dashboard/inflight`)**: every touch step in value mode rebuilt the whole surface on release to bring the encoder focus back, which stood the screen still for most of a second in flight and, on EdgeTX without its LVGL clear fix, kept that build's memory. The focus now goes back without a rebuild: the reverse encoder ladder hides for two screen refreshes and the step buttons for one (the buttons blink once); the first tap after the encoder was turned, or a sweep that does not finish within four refreshes, still rebuilds. Other touch actions are unchanged.

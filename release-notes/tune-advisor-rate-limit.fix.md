- **Tune Advisor keeps a suggested rate within what the flight controller keeps (`flight_tuning/tune_advisor`)**:
  - A rate change is checked against the flight controller's limit for the rate type (Actual: Center Sens and Max Rate 2000 °/s; Quick: Max Rate 2000 °/s; Rotorflight: Rate 1000 °/s, as the Rates page shows them) instead of the 255 a rate byte can hold. A change past it is no longer offered, and the reasons say the change needed goes past a setting's limit. Before, Apply wrote the value, the flight controller trimmed it on save, and the page reported "Saved, but the read-back differs" with F lowered and the rate only partly raised (#1142).

- **Battery, Sources and SmartFuel report the flight-controller save outcome (`setup/power/battery`, `setup/power/sources`, `setup/power/smartfuel`)**:
  - All three pages use the shared save for their writes and one EEPROM commit, reporting success only after the flight controller stores the values and reporting failed writes instead of saying Saved when queued. Battery sends a changed profile before its configuration, and all three pages read back after success while still open. Battery and SmartFuel model preferences are still saved on the radio before the flight-controller save starts, with a separate warning if that local save fails (#594).
  - On firmware with SmartFuel, Battery reads the flight controller's SmartFuel configuration after its load and sends it back unchanged after the battery configuration, as Rotorflight Configurator does, so a new minimum or full cell voltage applies to the flight controller's SmartFuel estimate at once instead of after a restart (#996).
